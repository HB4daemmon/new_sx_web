Enemy identity: EN-A1-C3, “灰潮火螭” (gray-tide fire chi-dragon). Draw a nimble, long-bodied feathered dragon-lizard with a narrow horned head, four small clawed feet, a connected sweeping tail, and layered ash-gray plumage with subdued ember-red feather tips. It should read as a grounded serpentine bird-reptile hybrid, not a western dragon and not a floating flame. Distinct from the otter and turtles; no wings large enough to lift it off its shared ground line.
