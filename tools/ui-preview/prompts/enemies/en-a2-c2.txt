Enemy identity: EN-A2-C2, “断桥回气魈” (broken-bridge echo-qi kui). Draw a compact, hunched ash-gray spirit yokai wrapped in a short layered mantle, with a hollow but friendly-readable mask face, one curved horn, dangling cord knots and one small solid brass bell attached at the collar. Give it wrapped forearms and unusually long sleeves that frame its body. It remains a single grounded figure, not a fog cloud; the bell is a costume object, never floating. Distinct from a human, bird or reptile.
