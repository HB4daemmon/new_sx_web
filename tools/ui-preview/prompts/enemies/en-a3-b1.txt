Enemy identity: EN-A3-B1, “毕方烈羽” (Bifang fierce-feather bird), an original single-legged firebird enemy. Draw a compact crane-like bird with one powerful visible leg and foot, a second leg absent, long balanced neck, hooked beak, broad layered cinnabar and blue-black wings, and a long blue-tipped tail. The bird stands on its single foot on the common ground line in every pose; it never flies. Use solid feathers, no flames, fire effects, extra legs or scenery.
